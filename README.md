### CHEM-000009: Addressable epimerization of every sugar stereocenter
- **Subfield:** Organic synthesis and medicinal chemistry
- **Open:** Invert a selected stereocenter of an unprotected sugar or chosen oligosaccharide residue while preserving all other centers and glycosidic linkages.
- **Status:** Selective rare-sugar epimerizations were demonstrated in 2020; 2026 kinetic-network control extended selected transformations, including GlcNAc-to-GalNAc. Independent access to every position in complex glycans remains unresolved.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Converts abundant sugars into otherwise costly stereochemical building blocks.
- **Key works:** Wang, Carder and Wendlandt (2020), *Synthesis of rare sugar isomers through site-selective epimerization*, [DOI:10.1038/s41586-020-1937-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-1937-1).
- **Related:** Applied mathematics: selective control of competing reaction-network steady states.
